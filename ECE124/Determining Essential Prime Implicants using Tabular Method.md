*Key Concepts:*
___

By creating a table with the collumns representing the required minterms and the rows representing the prime implicants, you can identify the essential prime implicants by looking at collumns with only one element in them.

This, in other words, is just looking for minterms which only one prime implicant covers (Ie, no other PIs can cover that PI's range). Therefore, it is an essential prime implicant.
