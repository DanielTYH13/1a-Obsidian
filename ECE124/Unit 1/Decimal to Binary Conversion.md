Q: How to convert from Decimal to Binary?

A:
For any given decimal, the algorithm the convert to binary is as follows:

1. Divide by 2, list the remainder.
2. Repeat step 1 with the quotient.
3. Repeat until quotient is 0.

The binary representation is listed as the remainders from lsb to msb.
