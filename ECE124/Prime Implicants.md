*Key Concepts:*
___

A prime implicant is one which no variables can be removed and still be a valid implicant. 

---

It is worth noting that in K-Map representation:

Maximally reduced implicants appear as larger groupings. Ie, coverage is inversely proportional to # of variables (There becomes more freedom with less restrictions).

For large functions, K maps become less useful. however, this is a useful visual intuition.
