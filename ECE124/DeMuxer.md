*Key Concepts:*
___

A de-muxer does the exact inverse of the muxer. It takes any input x, and ouputs it any $2^n$ lines for select line width of n bits.

---
The algebraic form of the demuxer:

$f = s_{1}' s_{0}' x + s_{1}' s_{0}x + \cdots$
