*Key Concepts:*
___

A Ripple full adder are full adders, which their carry outputs linked to the carry inputs of the subsequent full adders. Output is extracted through the sum, which are outputted in parallel.

Since each full adder must calculate the carry out using some gate delay, and pass it on, this is a slow implementation of the n bit full adder. It is also why it is called a ripple adder.
