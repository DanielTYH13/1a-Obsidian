*Key Concepts:*
___

The Johnson counter produces:

0000, 1000, 1100, 1110, 1111, 0111, 0011, 0001, 0000, etc:

![[Pasted image 20260620185302.png]]

Ie, it propogates some bit based on the complement of the last bit. When all are 0, it propogates 1s until all are 1s, after which is propogates 0s.
