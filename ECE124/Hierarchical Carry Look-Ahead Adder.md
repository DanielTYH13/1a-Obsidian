*Key Concepts:*
___

The hierarchical carry look-ahead adder combines ripple adders and CLA adders. 

The essence is that it ripples carries from chunks of CLAs instead of individual full adders. This way, we can reduce the average gate delay for each carry, while keeping gate fan in at an acceptable amount.
