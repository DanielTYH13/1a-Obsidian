*Key Concepts:*
___

Fan in is the number of inputs to a single gate. In practice, it become unpractical to have a large amount of gates.

This can be solved by 

1. Functional Decomposition
2. Factoring.

Both methods start by factoring out variables from the function expression.

In the case of factoring, this decomposes large gates into chained ones.
