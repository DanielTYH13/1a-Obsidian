*Key Concepts:*
___

- Given the square matrix A, then A is in:
	- Lower triangle form $\iff$ all entries with i < j are 0. Ie. When all the entries below the main diagonal are 0.
	- Upper triangle form $\iff$ all entries with i > j are 0. Ie. When all the entries above the main diagonal are 0.
	- Double triangle form if both the above are true.

- Given any triangle form, det(A) = $\displaystyle \prod^n_{i = 1} a_{ii}$
