*Key Concepts:*
___

- Addition follows normal matrix laws.
- Multiplication by a scalar follows normal matrix laws. 
- Given two diagonal matrices, the product is just each entry multiplied with its corresonding entry. 
	- Also follows normal laws, just is a shorcut. 

- Note: Diagonal multiplication is commutative. That is,
	- DF = FD for D, F are diagonal matrices.
