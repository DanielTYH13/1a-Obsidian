*Key Concepts:*
___

- Def'n of a matrix transformation: Let $A \in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{R})$, then for any given vector in $\mathbb{R}^n$, $f_A$ transforms the vector from one domain to its codomain:
	- $\mathbb{R}^n$ $\to$ $\mathbb{R}^m$
- By $f_{A}(\vec{x}) = A\vec{x}$. 
	- As you can see, the domain and codomains are defined by the dimensionality of the matrix A.
	- f "maps" x to Ax.
