*Key Concepts:*
___

- Given S, T be linear transformations, then the composition:
	- $(S \circ T)(\vec{x}) = S(T(\vec{x}))$

![[Pasted image 20251203133214.png|500]]

- By the definition of matrix transformations, this looks like this:
	- $[S]([T]\vec{x})$, and by the properties of matrix multiplication, then we have:
	- For all $S \in M_{pxn}(\mathbb{R})$, $T \in M_{nxm}(\mathbb{R})$, $[S\circ T] = [S][T]$
	- Note that $[S\circ T] \ne [T \circ S]$ from [[Properties of the Matrix Product]]
