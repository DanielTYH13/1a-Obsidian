*Key Concepts:*
___

- Given $T, S, R: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m$ by linear transformations, $c,d\in \mathbb{R}$,
	1. $T+S: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m$ Is linear: Notice how the domain/codomains are preserved.
	2. $cT: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m$ Is linear: Notice how the domain/codomains are preserved. 
	3. $T + S = S + T$
	4. $(T + S) + R = T + (S + R)$
	5. $c(dT) = (cd)T$
	6. $(c+d)T = cT + dT$
	7. $c(T+S) = cT + cS$
