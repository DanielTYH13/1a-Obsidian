*Key Concepts:*
______

- Given two matrices A, B $\in M_{mxn}(\mathbb{R})$, then if:
	- B is obtained from A by switching two distinct rows or columns in A, 
		- det(B) = -det(A)
	- B is obtained by adding a scalar multiple of one row or column from A to another distinct row or collumn, then
		- det(B) = det(A).
	- B is obtained from multiplying a row or column in A by $c\in \mathbb{R}$,
		- det(B) = cdet(A)
