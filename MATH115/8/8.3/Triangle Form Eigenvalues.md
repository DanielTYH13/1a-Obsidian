*Key Concepts:*
___

- Theorem: Given a matrix in [[Upper and Lower Triangle Form]], we have that $(A - I\lambda)$ is just the matrix with each entry on the diagonal subtracted by lambda.
	- Thus, $C_a(\lambda) = det(A - \lambda I ) = \displaystyle \prod^n_{i=1}(a_{ii}-\lambda)$
	- And thus, the eigenvalues are the values along the diagonal. 

- Theorem: Let $A\in M_{nxn}(\mathbb{R})$. For any eigenvalue $\lambda$ of A, 
	- $1 \le g_{\lambda} \le a_{\lambda} \le n$
