*Key Concepts:*
___

A series of the form $\displaystyle \sum^{\infty}_{k = 0} r^k$ converges if and only if $|r| < 1$, and has the series sum of $\frac{1}{1-r}$.

---
Derivation.

We know that the form of the partial sum is given as $\frac{1 - r^k}{1 - r}$, $r \ne 1$.

Taking the limit of the partial sum as k to infinity:

$\displaystyle \lim_{k \to \infty} \frac{1-r^k}{1-r}$
$\frac{ \displaystyle \lim_{k \to \infty} 1-r^k}{1-r}$

$= \infty, r>1$
$= \text{undefined}, r < -1$
$= \text{undefined}, r = 1, -1$
$= \frac{1}{1-r}, |r| < 1$
