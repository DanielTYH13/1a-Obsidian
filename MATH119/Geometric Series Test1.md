*Key Concepts:*
___

If a sequence is of form $a_{n} = ar^n$, then it is convergent to $\frac{a}{1 -r}$ if and only if $|r| < 1$. 

---

For a sequence of form $a_{n} = ar^n$, then the series up until n is given as:

$\displaystyle \sum^n_{k = 0} = \frac{a(1-r^{n+1})}{1-r}$

Thus, the infinite series for a geometric sequence is given as:

$\displaystyle \lim_{n = \infty} \sum^n_{k = 0} \frac{a(1-r^{n+1})}{1-r} = s$

Exercise, show that:

$s = \frac{a}{1-r}$ for $|r| < 1$
s DNE for $|r| \ge 1$
