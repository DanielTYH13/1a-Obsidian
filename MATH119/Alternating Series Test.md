*Key Concepts:*
___

The alternating series says that for any given alternating series of form $\sum (-1)^{n-C}a_n$, then if the sequence $a_{n}$:

- Tends to 0 as n goes to infinity
- Is monotonically decreasing

Then the series is convergent.
