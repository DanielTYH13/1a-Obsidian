*Key Concepts:*
___

For functions which have Taylor Polynomial error functions which tend to 0 as the terms go to infinity for all approximations of x along its domain, we can say that the Taylor Polynomial will be an exact representation of the function at an infinite degree.

Thus, we can represent the function as an infinite sum. Or in other words, the series of a sequence. In this case, the sequence are the terms of the taylor polynomial.

We call this the Taylor Series of f(x), and if centered at 0, the Mclaurin series of f(x).
