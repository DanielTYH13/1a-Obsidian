*Key Concepts:*
___

The P series test applies to series of form

$\frac{1}{n^p}$

The series $\displaystyle \sum^{\infty}_{n = 0}\frac{1}{n^p}$ is convergent if and only if $|p| > 1$

This can be proved using the integral test.
