*Key Concepts:*
___

Sequences are lists of numbers or terms, typically following some well defined rule.

Eg, $a_{n}=(-1)^n$, $a_{n}= \frac{1}{n^2}$

---
Sequence of Components $\{a_{n}\}$

The typical "base" sequence. What you evaluate a series of. 

---
Sequence of Partial Sums $\{s_{n}\}$

The value of partial sums up to n. 

$s_{n} = a_{0} + a_{1} + \cdots + a_{n}$
