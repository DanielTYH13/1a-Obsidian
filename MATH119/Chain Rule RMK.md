*Key Concepts:*
___

Chain rule exists because when you differentiate, you differentiate to a single variable, and the functions rate of change relative to it only. 

So when you sub in a function into another, you must incorporate the function's rate of change times the original functions rate of change. 

This is why it is of the form $\frac{d}{dx}f(g(x)) = f'(g(x))g'(x)$.

The derivative of f(x) at g(x), times the derivative of g(x) at x.

$\frac{d}{dt}e^{t^2} \ne e^{t^2}$
