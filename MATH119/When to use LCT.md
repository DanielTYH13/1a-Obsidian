*Key Concepts:*
___

LCT, at its core, is a comparison machine which checks if one series behave similarily to another, just scaled.

Thus, a strategy to realize that you must use LCT is to identify the [[Shape of Series|Asymptotic shape of the series]], meaning what series it looks like for very high terms, then using LCT with the defining series to see whether it is a scaled version of it or not.
