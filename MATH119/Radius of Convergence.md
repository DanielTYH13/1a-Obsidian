*Key Concepts:*
___

For a function f(x) which can be defined as a power series around some $x_{0}$, then the radius of convergence is the distance from $x_{0}$ to either endpoints of the convergence interval. Hence why it is called the radius of convergence.

$([x_{0} - R , x_{0} + R])$

In a more general case, it is the total magnitude of the interval divided by two. 

---
Derivation.

Usually, when you derive the radius of convergence using the ratio test, you will end up with an absval function. This is representative of the distance of x from some other constant $x_{0}$. Geoemtrically, what it is telling you is that the function is convergent as long as it is less than some distance, R, from the constant, $x_{0}$.
