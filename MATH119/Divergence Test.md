*Key Concepts:*
___

For a sequence $a_n$, if $\displaystyle \lim_{n = \infty} \ne 0$, then the series of that sequence is divergent. 

The logic is pretty straight forward. The infinite sum of finite terms will diverge. 

---

The inverse is not true, however. If the limit is 0, it can be both divergent or convergent. 

There are many examples of convergent series with a limit of 0. As for divergence, the harmonic sequence tends to 0, but the harmonic series diverges.

This is supposedly because it doesn't shrink fast enough for it to be finite??

Weird shit. Exercise, understand why.
