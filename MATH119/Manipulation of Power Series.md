*Key Concepts:*
___

If you have a function, that is equal to a power series with convergence radius R, that is equal to its expansion, then you can:

- Differentiate it
- Integrate it 
- Multiply through by a constant
- Add it to another series of a convergence radius $\ge R$

And maintain equality as well as the radius of convergence R.

---
This means that you can derivate the taylor or mclaurin series for many hard-to-differentiate functions by first finding the series for a simpler version, then differentiating, integrating, etc to get to the next one.
