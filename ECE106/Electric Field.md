*Key Concepts:*
___

Electric field is the force enacted by a charge per charge $\frac{N}{C}$. 

Thus, we have $\vec{E} = k \frac{Q}{r^2}\hat{r}$

---
Using cartesian coordinates, we have:

$r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$
$\hat{r} = \frac{x\hat{x} + y \hat{y}}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}}$

$\vec{E} = k\frac{Q}{(x^2 + y^2)^\frac{3}{2}}(x\hat{x} + y\hat{y})$
$a = k\frac{Q}{(x^2 + y^2)^\frac{3}{2}}$
$|\vec{E}| = \sqrt{a^2x^2 + a^2y^2}$
$|\vec{E}| = a\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$
$|\vec{E}| = k\frac{|Q|}{x^2 + y^2}$

---
Sum of electric fields

The combined electric field of multiple charges is just the sum of the electric field of each of each charge. This is caleld the component of the electric field from any given charge.
