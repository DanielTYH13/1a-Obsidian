*Key Concepts:*
___

For any three vectors $\vec{a}, \vec{b}, \vec{c}$ where 

$\vec{a} = \vec{b} \times \vec{c}$, then the vectors (By the definition of the cross product) can be represented as:

$\vec{a}$ by your thumb.
$\vec{b}$ by your index.
$\vec{c}$ by your middle finger. 

Note that a property of the cross product is that while $\vec{b}\cdot \vec{c}$ is not necessarily 0, $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 0$ and $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{c} = 0$
