*Key Concepts:*
___

It is a given that for a charged conductive surface, the E field is normal at all points along the surface (The tangential component of the E field at the surface is 0).

---
Proof by contradiction:

If the surface had non normal vectors, there would be some tangential component. Thus, there would be a force on some other charges around the tangential component. Thus, the system would be out of equillibrium. 

The system is in equilibrium, and thus proven.
