*Key Concepts:*
___

It is a given that all conductors have ZERO electric field within it. 

Moreover, the tangential component of the E field on the surface of the conductor is 0. This is required for the system to be in equilibrium.

Since voltage change is defined as $\displaystyle -\int^b_{a} \vec{E} \cdot \overline{dl}$, then since $\vec{E} \cdot \hat{l} = 0$ everywhere, there is no voltage change across any two points on an ideal conductor, and thus the entire conductor is equipotential.
