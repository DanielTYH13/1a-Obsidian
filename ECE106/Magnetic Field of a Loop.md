*Key Concepts:*
___

If you integrate the total magnetic field along the axis of a ring, it comes out to be:

$\vec{B} = \frac{\mu_0}{2} \frac{IR^2}{(R^2 + z^2)^{\frac{3}{2}}} \hat{z}$

---

If you set z = 0, you get:

$\vec{B} = \frac{\mu_{0}}{2}\frac{IR^2}{R^3} = \frac{\mu_{0}}{2}\frac{I}{R}$

A good approximation for multiple stacked loops is that they exist within each other. Thus, the approximate magnetic field for a solenoid of N coils is given as:

$\vec{B} = \frac{\mu_{0}NI}{2R}$

---

If $z >> R$, then you get 

$\vec{B} = \frac{\mu_0}{2}\frac{IR^2}{z^3}\hat{z}$

Substituting in the [[Magnetic Dipole Moment of a Loop]], you get:

$\vec{B} = \frac{\mu_{0}I\bar{\mu}}{2\pi z^3}$
