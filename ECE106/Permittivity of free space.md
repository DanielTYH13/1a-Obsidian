*Key Concepts:*
___

Permittivity of space is how much the medium allows electric fields to pass through. 

It composes the constant k in [[Coulomb's Law]]:

$k = \frac{1}{4\pi \epsilon_{0}}$, where permittivity is given by $\epsilon_{0}$. As such, for a high permittivity, the electric force is less, and vice versa.
