*Key Concepts:*
___
The capacitance of a component is defined by the ratio of charge stored vs the voltage:

$c = \frac{Q}{\Delta V} = \frac{Q \epsilon}{d \rho} = \frac{AQ \epsilon}{d Q} = \frac{\epsilon A}{d}$

The unit is referred to as Farads

---

It is important as by the [[Capacitor Equivalence|nature of capacitance]], the voltage of the capacitor will match the voltage of the power source. 

Referring to the equation of capacitance:
- Higher permittivity = higher capacitance (Due to a lower electric field per charge)
- Higher area = higher capacitance (More charge required to provide the same surface charge density to provide the same E field)
- Higher distance = lower capacitance (More voltage drop per E field strength)
