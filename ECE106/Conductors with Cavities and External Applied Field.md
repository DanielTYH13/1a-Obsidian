*Key Concepts:*
___

Assume a conductor with a cavity and an external field.

We know that for conductors with cavities, the net field within the cavity must be zero. As there is no non-neutral distribution of charges along the surface that will yield a net field of 0, then we know that the internal surface is neutral.

![[Pasted image 20260621170419.png]]

In other words, the conductor shields the inside from any outside E fields.
