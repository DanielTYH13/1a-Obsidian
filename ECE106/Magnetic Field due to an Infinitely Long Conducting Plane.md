*Key Concepts:*
___

Assume an infinitely large plane which conducts a current in one direction. 

By symmetry, the vertical components of the magnetic field at any given point above or below the plane cancel out, leaving only a horizontal component. (Draw a line orthogonal to the plane to the observation point. Choose current at equidistance away from the line. They cancel out, going off to infinity).

To find the exact expression for the magnetic field at any given point above the plane, we can utilize ampere's law, and draw a closed loop around the current such that some components cancel out, and leave us with a line that we can manipulate to include any given point above or below the plane, and which is collinear with the magnetic field, such that we can easily relate the field to the current.

We draw a rectangle around the current. By the dot product, the side components are 0, leaving us with two lines. The field is constant at all point along the line due to symmetry. We can isolate for field wrt current, giving us:

Then, since the enclosed current and length of the line stays constant and proportional regardless of line length or height, we know that the magnetic field at any given point outside of the plane is constant.
