*Key Concepts:*
___

The reason why Guass and Ampere's laws dont break down when a non enclosed charge is brought together is because on side side, the charge / current cancels out and reduces the over all contribution to the closed integral. However, on the other side, it contributes and thus increases the dot product.

The decrease in dot product on one side and increase on the other balance out, thus causing the closed integral to remain the same.
