*Key Concepts:*
___

Line charge density:
- $\rho _{l} = Q / L$

For any dQ within a line charge, it is defined as $\rho _{l} dL$

The electric field from a dQ is = $\frac{k d Q}{R^2} \cdot \frac{x \hat{x} - y' \hat{y}}{\sqrt{x^2 + y'^2}}$
- we use x', y' to indicate the coordinates of the source point
