*Key Concepts:*
___

All field lines disperse from the source in a certain pattern. 

For example, in the sphere, they disperse equally in all directions, thus representing the falloff of the E field proportional to $\frac{1}{r^2}$

Opposingly, in the cylinder, they disperse only in one direction (Lateral sources maintain strength in one dimensions) and thus represent the falloff of E field proportional to $\frac{1}{r}$

In general, the density of field lines show the convergence of E field, usually corresponding with strength. The caveat is that field lines do not show the strength falloff per field line, so they can be misleading at times.
