*Key Concepts:*
___

The E Field for a spherical capacitor is given by:

$E = \frac{Q}{4\pi r^2 \epsilon}$

Which is exactly Coulomb's law, which makes sense because Gauss's law only depends on the charge within it, and the guassian sphere represents some distance r from the center. 

---

The voltage for a spherical capacitor is given by: 

$\Delta V = -\frac{Q}{4\pi \epsilon}(\frac{a-b}{ab})$

---

The capacitance for spherical capacitor is given by:

$c = 4\pi \epsilon \frac{ab}{b-a}$

Ie, the capacitance depends only on the distance between the two walls.
