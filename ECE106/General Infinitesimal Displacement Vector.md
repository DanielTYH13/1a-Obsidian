*Key Concepts:*
___

The general infinitesimal displacement vector is given by: $dr \hat{r} + r d\theta \hat{\theta} + r\sin{\theta} d\phi \hat{\phi}$

Each of the vector components are tangential to their respective circle, and by definition of the r vector (it is normal to the circle), the dot product for any two vectors is 0.
