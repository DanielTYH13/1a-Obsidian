*Key Concepts:*
___

E field of a parallel plate capacitor is given by:

$\vec{E} = \frac{Q}{A \epsilon} \hat{r}$

And can be derived with Gauss's law.

---

Voltage of a parallel plate capacitor is given by:

$V = Er = \frac{Qr}{A \epsilon}$

Since E is parallel with r, and constant.

---

Capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor is given by:

$C = \frac{A\epsilon}{r}$
