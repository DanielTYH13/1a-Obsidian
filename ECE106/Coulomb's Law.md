*Key Concepts:*
___

Charges impart forces upon each other, depending linearly on the amount of charge and inversely proportional to the radius. Thus, we have:

$\vec{F} = k \frac{Qq}{r^2}\hat{x}$

Where Q is the source charge, and q is the charge which the force acts upon. The force is measured in Newtons. 

---
Direction of charge:

By convention, the direction vector $\hat{x}$ points in the direction form Q to q (along the axis defined by the two points). 

This is so that if both charges are equal in polarity, the force pushes them apart, and equally, if the charges are opposed, then the force draws them together.
