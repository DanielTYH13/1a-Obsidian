*Key Concepts:*
___

Conservative fields are fields where, for a given particle, regardless of the path, will experience the same amount of the component of the field collinear to its path from one point to another. 

Ie, the line integral of the field from a to b is the same regardless of the path.

The relation depicting the line integral of any point in relation to another fixed point is a function $\iff$ the field is conservative.

---
Implications:

Voltage is exactly the second definition of a conservative field for the E field, taken at the infinite reference point.  

At any point, you have a voltage. At any point, there can only be one voltage. 

In other words, since Voltage is the equivalence, then if you go from any given voltage to another, for all paths the negative line integral of E must equal the Delta voltage.
