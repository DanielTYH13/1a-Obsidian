*Key Concepts:*
___

The ground acts like a very large conductor. Since it is so large, it has a practically infinite amount of charge to give or take. When it does give or take, there is very little changed field (Approximately 0) in the ground.

Since the ground is so large, the "pressure" in the ground is largely undisturbed by giving or receiving charges. Thus, it readily sinks or provides charge. 

Thus, for any conductor connected to ground, if there is a positive or negative charge, it is equalized to neutral by the ground and thus field goes to zero, and thus voltage goes to zero.
