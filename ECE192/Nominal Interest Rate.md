*Key Concepts:*
___

The nominal interest rate is the interest rate per subperiod multiplied by the number of subperiods per year. 

Ie we have that for a 1% interest rate per month as the $i_s$, then $i_{r} = 1 * 12 = 12\%$

This is highkey a stupid and misleading way of representing interest, because if you have compounding interest then money invested at $i_{s}$ will gain more total interest than the nominal interest suggests.
