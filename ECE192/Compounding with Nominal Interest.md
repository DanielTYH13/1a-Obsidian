*Key Concepts:*
___

For money compounded m times a year for M years with a nominal interest of $i_r$, then the future worth is given as:

$F = P(1 + \frac{i_r}{m})^{m\cdot M}$

This is because we have that $i_{s}= \frac{i_r}{m}$ and the total compounding periods for s is $m \cdot M$
