*Key Concepts:*
___

It is useful to ask the question of what the effective interest would look like in compound interest if the amount was reinvested infinitely often but nominal interest stayed the same. 

Ie, what happens when you take the limit of nominal interest as the number subperiods go to infinity?

This is given as $\displaystyle \lim_{m \to \infty} (1 + \frac{i_{r}}{m})^m$. This limit turns out to be $e^{i_{r}}$.
