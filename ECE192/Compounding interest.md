*Key Concepts:*
___

If, over some time, the interest earned is then reinvested into generating more interest, it compounds over time. This is given by:

$F = P(1 + i)^N$

Where i is the interest per period, and N is the number of periods. 

You can conceptualize this by expanding outwards the exponent to see that you constantly "reinvest" the money.
