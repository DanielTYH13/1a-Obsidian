*Key Concepts:*
___

This is just the voltage at the terminals of a power supply. Found in Norton equivalent by taking the voltage at the resistor for an open circuit.

It is useful for finding the equivalent required Norton current source. 

---
It is also the voltage found through a power supply if its terminals are open. For Thevenin it is literally the power supply voltage.
