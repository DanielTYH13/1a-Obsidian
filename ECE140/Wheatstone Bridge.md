*Key Concepts:*
___

The Wheatstone Bridge is two voltage dividers with their outputs connected.

It is an easy way to set up a set of equations to solve for an unknown resistance with high accuracy. 

Solving the wheastone bridge is easiest when you can adjust resistances s.t. the voltage difference is 0. In this case,

$0 =v_{s} \frac{R_x}{R_{x}+R_{2}} - v_{s} \frac{R_3}{R_{1}+R_{3}}$
$0 = \frac{R_x}{R_{x}+R_{2}} - \frac{R_3}{R_{1}+R_{3}}$
$0 = \frac{R_x(R_{1}+R_{3}) - R_{3}(R_{x}+ R_{2})}{(R_{x}+R_{2})(R_{1}+R_{3})}$
$0 = \frac{R_{x}R_{1} - R_{3}R_{2}}{(R_{x}+R_{2})(R_{1}+R_{3})}$
$0 = {R_{x}R_{1} - R_{3}R_{2}}$
$R_x = \frac{R_{3}R_{2}}{R_{1}}$
