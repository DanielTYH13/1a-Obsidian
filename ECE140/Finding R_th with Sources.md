*Key Concepts:*
___

With voltage / current sources in a power supply, they only offset the graph of V/I (Of which's slope is the resistance). 

Thus, you can ignore the voltage / current sources when finding resistance "Looking into" the terminals of a power supply: 

![[Pasted image 20260606140233.png]]

---
Dependant Sources:

Unlike independant sources, dependant sources increase / decrease current / voltage depending on the input. Ie, they do not always shift the graph by a constant value -> They may change the slope of the graph and thus the resistance.

Thus, you must remove independant sources, then take a test voltage and find the current with the dependant sources.
