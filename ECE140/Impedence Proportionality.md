*Key Concepts:*
___

Inductors ($Z = j\omega L$)
- $Z \propto \omega$
- $\displaystyle \lim_{\omega \to 0} Z = 0$ (Looks like DC)
- $\displaystyle \lim_{\omega \to \infty} Z = \infty$ 

Capacitors ($Z = \frac{1}{j\omega L}$)
- $Z \propto \frac{1}{\omega}$
- $\displaystyle \lim_{\omega \to 0} Z = \infty$ (Looks like DC)
- $\displaystyle \lim_{\omega \to \infty} Z = 0$
