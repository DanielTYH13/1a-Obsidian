*Key Concepts:*
___

In some cases, you may not know where the true ground is (0V). Regardless, you are able to pick any given point and calculate voltage *in relation to* that point.

For example, in the following case:

![[Pasted image 20260625180819.png]]

if you choose the negative terminal at v_g, then you can calculate voltage relative to that point along i_a and the 80v source. 

In fact, in this case, this should be intuitive given that you are trying to find v_g, which is by definition the relative difference from the positive terminal to the negative.
