*Key Concepts:*
___

When dealing with AC signals, the RMS is the "apparent" voltage, current, etc. 

Example, think of PWM. While we send a square signal, the "apparent" voltage is given as the rms of the signal. It is what dc signal the AC signal behaves to be.

---

The rms for a sinusoidal signal is $\sqrt{2}f_{peak}$
