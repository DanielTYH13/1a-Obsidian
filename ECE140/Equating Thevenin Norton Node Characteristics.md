*Key Concepts:*
___

In the case of Norton and Thevenin equivalents, the theorem garuntees that the behaviour at the terminals is the same for any linear circuit attached to it. This means that for something like this circuit:

![[Pasted image 20260614164950.png]]

Since by KCL, $i_x$ = $I_{terminals}$, and since NT equivalent garuntees that $I_{terminals}$ stays the same, $i_x$ can be found by converting into a Norton circuit and then finding the norton current *under load.* 
	
(It is important to distinguish that $I_{norton}$ is just a default configuration to find the current when the power supply is *not* under load).
