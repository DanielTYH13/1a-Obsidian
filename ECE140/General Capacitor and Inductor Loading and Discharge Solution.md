Capacitor
- $v(t) = v_{final} + (v_{initial} - v_{final})e^\frac{t}{\tau}$
- $\tau = R_{eq}C$

Inductor 
- $I(t) = I_{final} + (I_{initial} - I_{final})e^\frac{t}{\tau}$
