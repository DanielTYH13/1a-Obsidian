*Key Concepts:*
___

KVL stands for Kirchkoffs voltage law, and dictates that :: the algebraic sum of voltage drops over a closed loop must be 0.

Imagine a single charge travelling through a closed loop, leaving some voltage $V_{0}$ and returning to the same point. In order for it to return to the same point, it must necessarily have a net voltage change of 0 relative to its initial voltage. 

KVL is useful for deriving equations to solve for unknowns.
