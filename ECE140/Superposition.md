*Key Concepts:*
___

In linear circuits, by definition, the "output" / characteristics of the circuit are linear functions of the input. Ie:

$V_{out}(af(x) + bg(x)) = aV_{out}(f(x)) + bV_{out}(g(x))$. 
							
This means that you can evaluate a circuit with only one voltage / current source multiple times, and sum the outputs for each case:

![[Pasted image 20260606135838.png|300]]

It is important that you leave dependant sources alone. They are part of the $V_{out}()$ function, and are not inputs.
