*Key Concepts:*
___

KCl stands for Kirchkoffs Current Law.

It dictates that :: the algebraic sum of currents flowing into a node must be 0. (What flows in must flow out).

Again, this is under the assumption that there is not charge remaining in the node. 

KCl is useful for deriving equations to solve for unknowns.
