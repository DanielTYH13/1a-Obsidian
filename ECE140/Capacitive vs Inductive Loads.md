*Key Concepts:*
___

In an RLC, the reactance is said to be:

Capacitive, if the reactance is negative. In other words, the phase shift from current to voltage is negative.

Inductive, if the reactance is positive.  In other words, the phase shift from current to voltage is positive.

---
Why?

Because capacitive loads lag voltage, and inductive loads lag current.
