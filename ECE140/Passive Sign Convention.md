*Key Concepts:*
___

The passive sign convention states that all voltage drops and currents should be measured from positive to negative terminals.

All it is is a convention, that ensures that you represent voltages and currents properly. This way, all physical laws, like KVL and KCL are properly represented in math.

---
Example, KVL:

In KVL, we we subtract sources which current passes negative to positive, and vice versa. This just establishes that the measured voltages are represented as increasing or decreasing voltage properly. 

Whether a source genuinely is stepping up or down voltage is completely irrelevant. As a source which is stepping up can be represented as a negative stepping down source. 

All the values and laws are necessarily existant in the math. Itsj ust that you must bookkeep properly so that parity of voltage is added or subtracted appropriately.
