*Key Concepts:*
___

The rise and fall times of an rc circuit is the time measured to go from 10 percent of VDD to 90 percent of VDD, and vice versa respectively. 

You can derive an exact expression for it, but it is often approximated as $2.2 \tau$. 

Since Tau is given as RC, then the rise and fall times are proportional to the resistance and the capacitance of the RC circuit. 

The standard of 10 to 90 is used in the majority of industrial applications.
