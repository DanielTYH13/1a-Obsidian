*Key Concepts:*
___

Current is always measured from + to -

Voltage drop is always measured from + to - (Ie with the flow of positive current)

---
We know that P = VI, $P > 0$ means absorbing power, $P < 0$ means developing power. This is because when the current opposes voltage drop, the signs will mistmatch and it will be negative.

Whenever current and voltage drop are aligned (Even if voltage drop is negative and current is negative), then power is positive and absorbed.

---
Important

Never assume a power supply is delivering power. An ideal power supply ONLY tells you the voltage / current at a point, nothing else. A PS is more well named as a voltage / current regulator.
