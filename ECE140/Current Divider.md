*Key Concepts:*
___

Current dividers work off the principle that a ratio of resistance in parallel with cause the current to differ. 

The equation for current down a branch is given as:
$I_{0}= \frac{I_{in} R_{1}}{R_{0} + R_{1}}$

![[Pasted image 20260530154859.png]]
---
Derivation:

$I_{0} = \frac{V_{in}}{R_0}$
$V_{in}=\frac{I_{in}}{R_{tot}}$
$V_{in}= \frac{I_{in}}{\frac{1}{R_1}+\frac{1}{R_0}}$
$V_{in}= \frac{I_{in}}{\frac{R_{0}+R_{1}}{R_{1} R_{0}}}$
$I_{0}= \frac{I_{in}}{\frac{R_{0} + R_{1}}{R_1}}$
$I_{0}= \frac{I_{in} R_{1}}{R_{0} + R_{1}}$
