Consider a branching node:

![[Pasted image 20260613181612.png]]

From the left is I_1, to the right is I_2. Then, by KCL, the down branch must be I_1 - I_2. 

Then, by KCL, I_1 - I_2 - (I_1 - I_2) = 0

As such, the mesh analysis method maintains KCL because the currents subtract precisely at the nodes where the two loops meet.
