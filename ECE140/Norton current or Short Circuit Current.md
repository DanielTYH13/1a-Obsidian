*Key Concepts:*
___

This is just the current at the terminals of a power supply. Found in Thevenin equivalent by taking the current of a short circuit at the terminals. 

It is useful for finding the equivalent required Thevenin voltage source. 

---
It is also the current found through a power supply if its terminals are shorted. For Norton it is literally the power supply current
