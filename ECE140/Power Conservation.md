*Key Concepts:*
___

The sum of instantaneous power over all components is always 0.

$∑_{k}v_{k} ​i_{k}​=0$

In a constant / stable state, this collapses to say that:

$p_{supplied} - p_{consumed} = 0$

This makes some cases of finding power supplied easier, as you are able to find the power absorbed by resistors, or vice versa, and equate to its counterpart.
