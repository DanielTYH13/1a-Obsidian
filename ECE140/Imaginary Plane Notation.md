*Key Concepts:*
___

We have that for any imaginary number $K = a + ib$, then we have:

$a = r \cos \theta$
$b = r \sin \theta$
$r = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$

Thus, $K = re^{i\theta}$ 

We introduce a new notation, $r \angle \theta$, which denotes a line of length r in the imaginary plane with angle $\theta$.

Thus, we have:

$K = r e^{i\theta} = r(\cos \theta + i\sin \theta) = r \angle \theta$, since $\cos \theta + i \sin \theta$ is a unit line in the imaginary plane with angle $\theta.$

---
Dividing angle / complex numbers

We also have:

$\frac{r_{1} \angle \theta_{1}}{r_{2} \angle \theta_{2}} = \frac{r_{1}e^{i \theta_{1}}}{r_{2}e^{i \theta_{2}}} = \frac{r_{1}}{r_{2}} e^{i(\theta_{1} - \theta_{2})} = \frac{r_{1}}{r_{2}}\angle (\theta_{1} - \theta_{2})$
