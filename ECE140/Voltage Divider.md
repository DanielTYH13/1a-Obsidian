*Key Concepts:*
___

A voltage divider works off the principle that two resistors in series will cause differing voltage drops based on the ratio of their resistance. 

Given n resistors in series, the voltage drop across a given resistor is $V_{out}= \frac{R_0}{R_{t}} \cdot V_{in}$

![[Pasted image 20260530153831.png]]

---
Derivation:

$V_0 = I_{in} R_0$
$I_{in} = I_{t}= \frac{V_{in}}{R_t}$
$V_{0} = \frac{V_{in}R_{0}}{R_{t}}$
