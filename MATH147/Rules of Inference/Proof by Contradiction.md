*Key Concepts:*
___

```ad-definition

Given the statement p is true, and you can prove that the negation of q implies the negation of p, then q is also true. 

$p \wedge (\neg q \implies \neg p) \implies q$

```

Formally, this can be shown from the contrapositive and [[Modus Ponens]]:
$$
\small
\begin{align}
&p \wedge (\neg q \implies \neg p) &\implies& q \text{\quad The contrapositive} \\
&p \wedge (p \implies q) &\implies& q  \text{\quad Modus Ponens}
\end{align}
$$
