*Key Concepts:*
___

- The MVT for integrals says that for the function f(x) continous on the closed interval $[a, b]$, then f(x) attains f(c) $c\in[a, b]$ s.t.:
	- $f(c)(b-a) = \displaystyle \int^b_{a}f(x)\mathrm{d}x$.

- Corrolorary:
	- We now have have that 
		- $A_{rect} = A_{1}+A_{3}$ 
		- $A_{curve} = A_{2}+A_{3}$
	- Where $A_{rect}$ is the area bound by the rectange $f(c)(b-a)$, $A_1$ is the area bound by $y =f(c), f(x)$ (Under), $A_2$ is the area bound by $y=f(c), f(x)$ (over), and $A_3$ is the area bound by $f(x), y =c, y =0$. 
		- We have MVT for integrals: $f(c)(b-a) = \displaystyle \int^b_{a}f(x)\mathrm{d}x  \implies A_{rect} = A_{curve}$ 
		- $A_{1} + A_{3} = A_{2}+ A_3$
		- $A_{1} = A_{2}$
	- Or in other words, the area bound by $f(x), y = f(c)$ above and below are equal to each other.
