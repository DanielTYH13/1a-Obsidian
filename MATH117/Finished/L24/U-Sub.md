*Key Concepts:*
___
- U sub is a method of integration. It involves replacing complex expressions by a single variable, and then trying to represent the integral in terms of that new variable:
	- This is typically done with the variable u, hence the name. 

- Definition:
	- Given u = f(x), then:
		- $\displaystyle \int f(g(x))g'(x)dx$
		- $\displaystyle \int f(u) \frac{du}{dx} dx$
		- $\displaystyle \int f(u) {du}$
	- Can you see how this stems from chain rule? Given that u is a function, then:
		- $f(u)du = f'(u)u'dx$
