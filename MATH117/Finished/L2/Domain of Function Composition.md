*Key Concepts:*
___

- When composing the function $(f\circ g)(x)$, the resulting domain of the function is ==$S = \{x\in \mathbb{R} | x \in D_{f(x)}, f(x) \in D_{g(x)}\}$==
	- S is the ==subset of $D_{f(x)}$ which is "mapped" to $D_{g(x)}$ by $f(x)$.==
