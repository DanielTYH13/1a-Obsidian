**Evaluating Inequalities in Cases**

- Given f(x), g(x) are piecewise functions, then the key strategy to evaluate inequalities between the two is
%%  %%
	- Find points where the function behaviour changes (Critical points)
	- Determine the intervals which lie in between the critical points.
	- Substitute the correct function definition at for the first interval
	- Determine the inequality
	- Find the intersection between the interval and the solution of the inequality.
	- Repeat for all intervals.
	- The answer is the union of the restricted solutions for all cases. 

---
**Reasoning**

- This algorithm reduces the piecewise inequality into normal inequalities with restricted domains. Since each respective solution is valid only under the domains of each case, we take an intersection.
