*Key Concepts:*
___

- The [[General Form of a Conic Section]] for a circle is:
	- $\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y - k)^2}{a^2} = 1$
	- $(x-h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = a^2$
	- This is directly ==pythagorean theorem of form $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$==, where the coords are the height and width. Since c is ==a constant, then this defines a circle!==
		- Ie, only points that are ==a certain radius away from the midpoint will satisfy the equation== - thus creating a circle.
