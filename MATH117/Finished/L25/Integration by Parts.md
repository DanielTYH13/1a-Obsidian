*Key Concepts:*
___

- Derivation:
	- $\frac{d}{dx}[vu] = u'v + uv'$
	- $\displaystyle \int \frac{d}{dx}[vu \mathrm{d}x] = \int (u'v + uv')$
	- $\displaystyle [vu] = \int u'v\mathrm{d}x + \int uv'\mathrm{d}x$
	- $\displaystyle \int u'v \mathrm{d}x =  [vu] - \int uv'\mathrm{d}x$, or in shorthand: (Oops, v & u should be reversed)
	- $\displaystyle \int udv  =  [vu] - \int vdu$: The idea being that the right handed integral will be simplified. (This is the correct ordering!)
