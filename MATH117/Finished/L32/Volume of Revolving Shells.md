*Key Concepts:*
___

- In cases when we revolve around a vertical axis, it becomes difficult to define the volume in terms of perpendicular washers. Thus, we take "shells" instead:
	- $V = \displaystyle \int^b_{a}2\pi rhdx$, where in most cases:
		- r is the variable
		- h is the function:
	- $V = \displaystyle \int^b_{a}2\pi x f(x) dx$
