*Key Concepts:*
___

If $f: A \to B$ is a bijection, then so is $f^{-1}$, and $f^{-1^{-1}} = f$.

Be careful! The range is not the same thing as the codomain. So a function can be injective, and thus its inverse is a bijection from its range to its domain, but it is not necessarily a bijection from its codomain to its domain.
