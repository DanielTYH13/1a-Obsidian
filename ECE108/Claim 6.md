*Key Concepts:*
___

If a, b, c are positive integers, then at least one of:

$a - b$
$a -c$
$b - c$ 

Are even.

---
Proof:

For all even, then any differnce will be even.
For all odd, then any difference will be even 
For some odd, some even, then there exists at least a pair which are both even or odd. then the difference will be even.
