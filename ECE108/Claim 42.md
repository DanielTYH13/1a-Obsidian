*Key Concepts:*
___

$Pr\{A \bigcup B\} = Pr\{A\} + Pr\{B\} - Pr\{A \bigcap B\}$

---
Intuition:

Probability is found by counting cases. When two events are non mutually exclusive, you include the cases where both events occur simutaneously within Pr{A} and Pr{B}. Thus we double count, and must subtract from the sum.

---

The proof is left as an exercise.

Hint is to use $|A \bigcup B| = |A| + |B| - |A\bigcap B|$
