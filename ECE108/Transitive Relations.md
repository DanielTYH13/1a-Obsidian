*Key Concepts:*
___

A relation is transitive if, given mappings $\langle x, y \rangle, \langle y, z \rangle$, then there exists $\langle x, z \rangle$.

$\forall \langle x, y \rangle, \langle y, z \rangle \in A^2, \langle x, y \rangle \in R \wedge \langle y, z \rangle \in R \implies \langle x, z \rangle \in R$
