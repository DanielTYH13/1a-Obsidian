*Key Concepts:*
___

If there exists some injection from A to $\mathbb{N}_n$, for some $n \in Z^+_{0}$, then A is countable and finite. 

This is akin to "indexing" each of the elements of A one to one with another in $\mathbb{N}_n$, and since we know that any subset of $\mathbb{N}$ is countable and finite, we get that their cardinality are the same.
