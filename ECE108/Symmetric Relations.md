*Key Concepts:*
___

Symmetry of a relation is defined as $\forall \langle x_{1}, y_{1} \rangle \in A^2, \langle x_{1}, y_{1} \rangle \in R \iff \langle y_{1}, x_{1}\rangle \in R$

A relation is considered to be symmetric if for every ordered pair, its mirror image is in the relation. Ie, you can go both ways for any given ordered pair.
