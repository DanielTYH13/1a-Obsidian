*Key Concepts:*
___

If $f: A \to B$ is bijective, then $f^{-1}: Range(f) \to A$ is bijective, too. 

This is a direct consequence of [[Claim 25]], as bijectivity implies that the $Range(f) = Codomain(f)$.
