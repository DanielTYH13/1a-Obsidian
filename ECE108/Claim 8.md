*Key Concepts:*
___

Given two real numbers, $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$, there exists $z \in \mathbb{R} \implies x < z < y$.

---
Proof by construction.

Construct $z = \frac{x + y}{2} = \frac{x}{2} + \frac{y}{2}$
$x = \frac{x}{2} + \frac{x}{2}$
$y = \frac{y}{2} + \frac{y}{2}$

Therefore, 

$x < \frac{x+y}{2} < y$
