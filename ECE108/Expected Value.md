*Key Concepts:*
___

The expected value of a sample space is the sum of each event's probability times its random variable. 

In other words,

$E(S) = \displaystyle \sum_{x \in R}x Pr\{X = x\}$
$E(S) = \displaystyle \sum_{s \in S}X(s) Pr\{s\}$           Since $Pr\{X = x\} = \displaystyle \sum_{s \in S, X(s) = x} Pr\{s\}$
