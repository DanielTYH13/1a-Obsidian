*Key Concepts:*
___

Combinations are like permutations but where order does not matter.

The combination $nCr = \frac{nPr}{r!}$

---

Intution:

When you pick using factorial, you are including *all* ways to arrange the members. This includes all ways that you can "shift" around the members. 

Thus, you divide the permutation by the number of ways you can rearrange the set. 

Another way to think of it is to think that:

$nPr = r! \cdot nCr$

Since $nCr$ branches off of $r!$, which are the ways to arrange the set.
