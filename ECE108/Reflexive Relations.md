*Key Concepts:*
___

A relation is reflex if for all elements in the domain, we have that the ordered pair of the element to itself exists. 

$\forall x \in A, \langle x, x \rangle \in R$

---
Irreflexivity is defined as the opposite:

$\forall x \in A, \langle x, x \rangle \notin R$
