*Key Concepts:*
___

Relations are sets of ordered pairs which map one element to the other.

Order matters, hence the ordered pairs.

If the relation maps between sets B and C, then the relation is subset of the cartesian cross product $B \times C$. 

Oftentimes, you will see an larger set, denoting the set of all elements the relation is concerened about, which is $A = B \bigcup C$. Then, since $B \times C$ is a subset of $A^2$, the relation is also a subset of $A^2$.

---
Classifications of Relations:

- [[Symmetric Relations]]
- [[Asymmetric Relations]]
- [[Antisymmetric Relations]]
- [[Reflexive Relations]]

Posets are reflexive, antisymmetric, and transitive

Strict Partial Orders are irreflexive, asymmetric, and transitive. 

Equivalences are reflexive, symmetric, and transitive. 

---
Properties of relations:

Functions are a subset of relations. Ie, relations can sometimes be functions, and sometimes not. 

Relations are a subset of the cartesian cross product between their domain and codomain.
