*Key Concepts:*
___

For $S \ne \phi$, then if you can construct an injection $S \to \mathbb{N}$, then S is countable.

This is akin to labelling each element of S with a number.

Injections can either be bijections or just injections. 

If it is a bijection, then the cardinality of S is equal to N, or $\aleph_{0}$. 

If it is an injection, then the cardinality of S is equal to some subset of N (Ie the injection is a bijection for the codomain that is a subset of N), which we know to be finite and countable.
