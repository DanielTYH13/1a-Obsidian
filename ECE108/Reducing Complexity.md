*Key Concepts:*
___

A fundamental strategy in proofs is to find a seperate proposition which implies the claim you are trying to prove, but either requires more comprehensive premises or is easier to prove. 

For example, induction does this by shrinking the proof from a large domain to just the step.

Contradiction does this by flipping the domain of what you need to prove. Especially for forall claims, you prove just one logical inconsistency instead of proving the consistency of infinte elements.
