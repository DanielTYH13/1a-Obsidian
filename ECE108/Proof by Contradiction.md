*Key Concepts:*
___

Proof by contradiction stems from the fact that **the only case where** $p \implies q$ is false is the case where $\neg p \wedge q$.

Thus, if you can prove that $\neg p \wedge q$ is never true, then you can prove that $p \implies q$. Usually, this process follows by logical deduction.
