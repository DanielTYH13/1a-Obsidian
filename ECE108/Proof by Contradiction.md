*Key Concepts:*
___

Proof by contradiction stems from the fact that **the only case where** $p \implies q$ is false is the case where $p \wedge \neg q$.

Thus, if you can prove that $p \wedge \neg q$ cannot be true, then you can prove that $p \implies q$. Usually, this process follows by logical deduction by asserting that q is not true, then deducting to a contradiction to the premises.
