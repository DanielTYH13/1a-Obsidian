*Key Concepts:*
___

Proving divisibility often involves induction or case analysis. 

---
Induction

Induction is oftne used when you have a polynomial expression which is asserted to be divisible by a number. By isolating for the previous polynomial in the induction step, you can allow it be a multiple of an integer.

---
Case analysis.

Oftentimes, a number not divisible by the divisor is made to be divisible through a set of operations. In a trivial case, 2n is divisible by 2 because:

If n is divisible by two, then a multiple of n is divisible by 2.
If n is not divisible by two, or in other words expressable as $n' + 1$ where $n'$ is divisble by 2, then $2n = 2n' + 2$ is divisible by two. 

In other words, the remainder is manipulated to turn into a multiple of the divisor, thus maing it divisble by the divisor.
