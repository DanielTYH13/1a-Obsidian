*Key Concepts:*
___

- Impulse:
	- $\vec{F}_{net}\cdot \mathrm{d} t = \mathrm{d} p$
		- ==Exer: Derive this from the definition of P=mv and F=ma==
	- And thus, integrating gives us:
		- $\Delta p = \displaystyle \int^{t_2}_{t_{1}} \vec{F}_{net} \mathrm{d}t$
		- $\Delta p = \vec{F}_{avg} \mathrm{d}t$
			- This value is $\vec{I}$, or Impulse.
