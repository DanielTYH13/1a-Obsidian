*Key Concepts:*
___

- When finding the eigenvalues of a matrix $A \in M_{mxn}(\mathbb{R})$ ([[Archive/MATH115/8/8.2/Finding Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors Algebraically]]), we set up:
	- $det(A - I \lambda) = 0$. 
- If you expand this, it becomes a polynomial. Thus, its roots are the eigenvalues.
- This is called the **Characteristic Polynomial** of any given matrix.
	- $C_{A}(\lambda) = det(A - I \lambda)$
	- For any matrix $M_{nxn}$, its characteristic polynomial will have degree n.
