*Key Concepts:*
___

```ad-theorem

If p implies s and q implies r, and we know either p or q is true, then either s or r are true. 

$[(p\implies s) \wedge (q \implies r) \wedge (p \vee q)] \implies (s \vee r)$

```

It is interesting to think how this can be realized from the [[Destructive Dilemma]] using the [[Law of the Excluded Middle]].
