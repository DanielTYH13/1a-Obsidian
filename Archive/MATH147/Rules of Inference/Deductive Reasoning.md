*Key Concepts:*
___

```ad-definition

Using general knowledge to conclude about something specific. 

```

Deductive reasoning shows that if the premises are true, then the conclusion must also be true. It allows for truths to be gradually built off of assumed truths (axioms), and is therefore often rigorous and used to write proofs.
