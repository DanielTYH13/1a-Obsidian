*Key Concepts:*
___

- Given $M \in M_{nxn}(\mathbb{R})$, M is diagonizable if and only if there exists a basis B for $\mathbb{R}^n$ that is composed of eigenvectors of M. 
	- This is true $\iff$ any of the following are true
		- $g_{i}= a_{i}$ for all $\lambda_{i}$ 
		- All eigenvalues for M are distinct 
	- This stems from [[Theorem 8.4.6]]
