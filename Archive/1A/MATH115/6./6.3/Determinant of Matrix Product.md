*Key Concepts:*
___

- Theorem: Given $A, B \in M_{nxn}(\mathbb{R})$, then:
	- $det(AB) = det(A)det(B)$
	- Note: 
		- det(AB) = det(A)det(B)=det(B)det(A)=det(BA), since determinants are scalar values. 
			- Thus, det(AB) = det(BA) even if $AB \ne BA$
		- $det(A^k) = (det(A))^k$
			- Proof left as an exercise
