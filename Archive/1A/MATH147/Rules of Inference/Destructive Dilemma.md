*Key Concepts:*
___

```ad-theorem

If we know that p implies s and q implies r, and either p or q are false, then either s or r are false. 

$[(p \implies s) \wedge (q \implies r) \wedge (\neg p \vee \neg q)] \implies (\neg s \vee \neg r)$

```

It is interesting to think how this can be realized from the [[Constructive Dilemma]] using the [[Law of the Excluded Middle]].
