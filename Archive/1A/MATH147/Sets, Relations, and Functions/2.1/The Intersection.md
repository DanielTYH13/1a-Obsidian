*Key Concepts:*
___

Given set $X$ and sets $A, B \in X$, 

```ad-definition

We define the intersection of sets A, B as

$\displaystyle \bigcap_{\alpha \in I} A_{alpha} = \{x | \forall \alpha \in I : x \in A_\alpha \}$

```
