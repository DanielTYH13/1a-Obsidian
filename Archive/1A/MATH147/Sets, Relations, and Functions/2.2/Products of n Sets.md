*Key Concepts:*
___
Given n sets $\{ X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{n}\}$,

```ad-definition

The product of $\{ X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{n}\}$ is defined by,

$X_{1} \times X_{2}\times \cdots \times X_{n} = \displaystyle \prod^n_{i = 1}X_{i} = \{(x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n}) | x_{i}\in X_{i}\}$

```
