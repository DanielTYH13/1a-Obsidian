*Key Concepts:*
___

Let $\{X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{n}\}$ be a finite collection of sets,

```ad-theorem

The Cardinality of the set product is given by:

$\left|\displaystyle \prod_{i=1}^n X_{i} \right| =\displaystyle \prod_{i=1}^n |X_i|$

```
