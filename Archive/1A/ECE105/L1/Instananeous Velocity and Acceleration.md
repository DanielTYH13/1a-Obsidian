*Key Concepts:*
___

- Velocity: ==$\displaystyle \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{\Delta \vec{x}}{\Delta \vec{t}}$==
- Acceleration: ==$\displaystyle \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{\Delta \vec{v}}{\Delta \vec{t}}$==

- Instantaneous Velocity/Acceleration is derivative of displacement/velocity wrt time. 
	- It is therefore natural to see it defined this way.

- When you see $\vec{v}$ or $\vec{a}$ with no other styling, it refers to instantaneous velocity.
