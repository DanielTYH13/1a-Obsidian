*Key Concepts:*
___

- SHM is motion when the restoring force is linear with displacement. 
	- We typically model this as a spring force, and use that to find the differential equation relating acceleration and position:
	- $F = ma$
	- $F = -kx$
	- $-kx = ma$
	- $ma + kx = 0$
	- $\frac{d^2x(t)}{dt}m + x(t)k = 0$

- Some solutions to this differential equation are:
	- $A\cos(\omega t + \phi)$
	- $A\sin(\omega t + \phi)$

- Contrary to SHM, pendulums have a restoring force that is NOT linearly proportional to displacement.
	- This is why we have to use the small angle approximation to turn it into a SHM problem, which from there we can then begin deriving understanding.

- Dont think of SHM as a physical object, instead think of it purely as a value that oscillates with a restoring force - like a value bar.
