*Key Concepts:*
___

- The integral for finding arc length of a function is given by:
	- $\displaystyle \int^b_{a}\sqrt{1 + \left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right )^2}dx$
- Similarily, for functions that take y as the input and x as the output:
	- $\displaystyle \int^b_{a}\sqrt{1 + \left(\frac{dx}{dy}\right )^2}dy$
	- Note, functions with y inverted for x are functionally identical, they are just graphed differently based on the the convention that we have for graphing.
