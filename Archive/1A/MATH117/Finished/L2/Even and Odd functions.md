*Key Concepts:*
___
**Definition:**

- Given f(x):
	- f(x) is even $\iff$ f(-x) = f(x)
	- f(x) is odd $\iff$ f(-x) = -f(x)

---
**Graphical Interpretation**:

- Even functions are symmetrical across x = 0.
- Odd functions are invariant under a rotation of $\pi$ around (0, 0)
	- Ie, -f(x) is obtained by reflecting f(x) across x = 0 and y = 0

- Fun Fact: The only function which is both even and odd is $y=0$
